Base durations are sampled log-uniformly. Pair order, Start-to-first-tick delay, ISI, and after-answer pause are sampled independently inside their configured bounds. Equal bounds produce fixed timing. With no ISI, the intervals share one middle boundary click and playback uses three clicks. Start-to-first-tick includes the 500 ms audio pre-roll.
